Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply both left-hand and right-hand locking gear sets?

Yes. Left-hand and right-hand configurations are available. Please confirm the required quantity for each direction.

Can the locking rod length be customized?

Yes. Rod length can be produced according to your confirmed dimensions, sample or technical drawing.

What is included in one complete set?

The component list may include locking rods, cams, keepers, handles, retainers, bushes and brackets. The final packing list is confirmed before production.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Send the left and right quantities, rod length or drawing, required surface finish, total quantity and destination.

Can you provide export packing for LCL shipments?

Yes. Components can be counted, labeled, bundled and palletized according to the shipment size and destination.

What is the production lead time?

Lead time depends on the specification, quantity and current production schedule. It will be confirmed with the quotation.

Can I arrange inspection before shipment?

Yes. Inspection requirements and photo records can be discussed before the order is confirmed.

What shipping terms are available?

FOB, CFR, CIF and other suitable options can be discussed after the cargo quantity, packing and destination are confirmed.
