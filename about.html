Who We Are

A Practical Supply Partner for Container Businesses

Centro Containers is based in Jiaozhou, Qingdao, one of China's established manufacturing and logistics regions. We focus on components used to build, maintain and repair ISO dry shipping containers.

Our team combines container product knowledge with steel fabrication resources to help professional buyers source standard parts and develop made-to-drawing components. From specification review to packing and shipment, we keep communication clear and commercially focused.
