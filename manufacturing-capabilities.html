Manufacturing processes

Core Processes for Custom Container Components

Production requirements vary by component. We review the drawing, material, geometry and batch quantity before confirming the most suitable manufacturing route.

Steel Cutting

Sheet and profile preparation for parts defined by a confirmed drawing and material specification.

Bending

Forming straight bends and component geometries where the part and batch suit the available route.

Forming

Corrugated panels and structural profiles made to confirmed dimensions, profile and material.

Welding

Welded structural components and assemblies produced against an agreed specification and inspection plan.
