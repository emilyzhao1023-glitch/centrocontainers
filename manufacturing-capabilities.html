Manufacturing processes

Core Processes for Custom Container Components

Build-to-print fabrication starts with the drawing or specification. We review material, geometry, critical features and batch quantity before selecting a practical production process for each container component.

Steel Cutting

Sheet and profile preparation for parts defined by a confirmed drawing and material specification.

Bending

Forming straight bends and component geometries where the part and batch suit the available process.

Forming

Corrugated panels and structural profiles made to confirmed dimensions, profile and material.

Welding

Welded structural components and assemblies produced against an agreed specification and inspection plan.
