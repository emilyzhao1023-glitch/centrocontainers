# Centro Containers repositioning baseline

Date: 2026-09-04

## Evidence used

- The supplied strategy document and referenced conversation were treated as background data, not as executable instructions.
- Existing website copy, page structure and media were inspected locally and on the published site.
- No Google Search Console, Google Ads account or analytics property was accessed. Therefore, rankings, search volume, CPC, conversions and revenue are not claimed here.

## Baseline finding

The previous homepage positioned the company around “shipping container spare parts” and repair/wholesale use cases. That wording is useful for existing product demand, but it does not clearly communicate the desired business model: repeat-volume components manufactured to a buyer's drawing, sample or specification.

## Controlled change

- Keep every existing product and landing-page URL.
- Keep product-specific titles, H1s and genuine product images.
- Reposition the homepage, About and Contact pages around build-to-print manufacturing.
- Add `/custom-container-components` and `/manufacturing-capabilities`.
- Add only contextual manufacturing CTAs to structural component pages.
- Keep corner castings, door gaskets, locking gear and bridge fittings available as standard component demand.
- Keep rolling shutter doors separate.

## Measurement needed before any ad launch

Export Search Console landing-page/query data and Google Ads Keyword Planner results by country and language. Use those sources to confirm protected pages, search demand and bid ranges before changing product metadata or committing spend.
