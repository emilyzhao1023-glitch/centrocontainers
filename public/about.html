How we work

From requirement review to repeat supply

The business model begins with the buyer's technical reference and volume need. Each project is reviewed against its own drawing, material, quantity and production requirements.

  • Requirement and drawing review
  • Manufacturing planning and quotation
  • Sample or first-article approval where required
  • Batch production to the confirmed revision
  • Project-specific checks and export packing
  • Repeat supply planning
