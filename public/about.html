FAQ

About Working With Us

What container parts does Centro Containers supply?

We supply standard ISO container parts, including corner castings, panels, door hardware, gaskets and structural components.

Can you support OEM production?

Yes. Our team can review drawings, materials, tolerances and order requirements for OEM steel component production.

How do you manage product quality?

Requirements are reviewed before production, with process checks and final inspection coordinated according to the product and order specification.

Can different parts be combined in one shipment?

Mixed loading can be planned for compatible products to help buyers consolidate container parts in one shipment.

Do you support international shipping?

Yes. We assist with export packing, loading plans, documents and coordination based on the destination and agreed trade term.
