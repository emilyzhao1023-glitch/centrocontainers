Buyer questions

Before you send an RFQ

Can you make my exact drawing?

Send the file, material, quantity and use case. Feasibility is confirmed only after review; the website does not promise that every drawing is manufacturable.

Can I approve a sample?

Where appropriate, a sample or first-article stage can be agreed before the full batch.

What is the MOQ?

There is no universal number. The economical minimum depends on part size, tooling, process and specification.

How is repeat production controlled?

The confirmed drawing or specification revision and approved reference define the order; project-specific QC and packing requirements should be agreed before production.

Can I send a drawing?

Yes. The RFQ form collects the project basics first, and confidential files can then be shared through an agreed email or secure channel.

What helps you quote?

Part description, drawing status, material, quantity per batch, annual volume, destination and timing.

Ready for a manufacturing review?

Send the commercial and technical basics first. If the project fits, the team can arrange the appropriate channel for drawings and samples.
