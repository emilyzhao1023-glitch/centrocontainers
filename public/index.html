The sourcing model

You provide the drawings. We manufacture the parts.

Centro Containers is positioned for overseas manufacturers, assemblers and procurement teams that own the drawing or sample and need stable repeat production—not only one-off replacement parts.

01

Drawing Review

Part, material, dimensions, quantity and destination are checked before quotation.

02

Manufacturing Plan

Suitable processes, tooling needs and economical batch size are reviewed.

03

Sample / First Article

A sample route can be agreed where the project requires approval before the run.

04

Approval

The agreed drawing, specification and approved reference define production.

05

Batch Production

Parts are produced to the confirmed revision and order requirements.

06

QC & Packing

Checks and packing are arranged against the agreed product and shipment plan.
