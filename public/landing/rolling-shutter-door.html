Buyer Questions

Rolling Shutter Door FAQ

Can the doors be made to custom opening sizes?

Yes. Send clear opening width and height, installation conditions and required quantity for review.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Provide opening size, application, preferred operation, color, quantity and destination. Photos or drawings are helpful.

Can operation testing be confirmed before shipment?

Testing, inspection and documentation requirements can be agreed with the order.

How are the doors prepared for export?

Packing is selected according to the confirmed configuration, shipment method and destination.

Can different sizes be included in one project?

Yes. List each opening size and the required quantity for that size in your inquiry.

Which trade terms are available?

FOB, CFR, CIF and other suitable options can be discussed for the project.
