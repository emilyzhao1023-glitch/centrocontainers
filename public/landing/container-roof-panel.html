FAQ

Container Roof Panel Questions

Can you supply roof panels for 20ft and 40ft dry containers?

Yes. Roof panels can be supplied for dry container repair, manufacturing and refurbishment. Final dimensions, profile and panel arrangement are confirmed against the buyer's drawing or specification.

Can container roof panel dimensions and profiles be customized?

Drawing-based OEM supply is available. Please provide the required length, width, thickness, corrugation profile and overlap details for feasibility and quotation review.

What information is needed for a roof panel quotation?

Please send the container type, drawing or dimensions, material and thickness requirement, surface finish, quantity and destination.

Can roof panels be packed for export shipment?

Yes. Steel pallet, plywood pallet or other suitable export packing can be discussed according to the panel size, order quantity and shipping method.
